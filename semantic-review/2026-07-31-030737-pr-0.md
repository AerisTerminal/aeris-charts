## Retained child observation invalidates the legend-owning parent

`InteractiveDemo::new` registers `cx.observe(&chart, |_, _, cx| cx.notify())` and stores the returned `Subscription` in `_root_observer`. Primary-cell protection prevents that observed root entity from being removed. Pointer movement updates the root `Probe` legend before its `cx.notify()`; the observation then notifies `InteractiveDemo`, whose render snapshots `probe.legend` from the observed entity for the visible overlay. This closes the previously identified stale-parent path.
